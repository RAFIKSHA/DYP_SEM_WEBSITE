About D. Y. Patil College

D. Y. Patil Group of Institutions is a prominent name in the field of education in India. Established with a vision to provide high-quality education and holistic development to students, the college offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs across various disciplines including Engineering, Management, Pharmacy, Architecture, and more.

With state-of-the-art infrastructure, experienced faculty, and strong industry ties, D. Y. Patil College has consistently maintained high academic standards and nurtured students to become responsible professionals and leaders in their respective fields. The college is known for its vibrant campus life, modern labs, well-equipped libraries, sports facilities, and an emphasis on research and innovation.

The institution's commitment to academic excellence and social responsibility continues to inspire thousands of students each year, as it works towards creating a better future through education.
